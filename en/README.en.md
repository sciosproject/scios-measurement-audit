# `en/` — English translations of the Arabic evidence

**Translation. The Arabic is authoritative and is the hashed artifact.**

These files exist because the report is written in English while the documents its claims
rest on were written in Arabic. A report that says "everything here is checkable" to a
reader who cannot read a single piece of its evidence has made a claim wider than what it
delivers — the exact defect the report documents.

**Where a translation and the Arabic disagree, the Arabic governs.** The freeze applies to
the Arabic files: they are the ones whose hashes are recorded, whose sections are dated,
and above which nothing is ever edited. A translation is a convenience for the reader, not
a second original — so translating cannot be used to alter a frozen text.

Quotations from third-party papers inside these translations are reproduced in their
original English, exactly as in the Arabic files, and are covered by the same automated
quote check.
